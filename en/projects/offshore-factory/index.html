Offshore Factory

2026.06
Overview
I designed an offshore factory around the theme of ice and industry. The player enters the damaged platform as an engineer and uses ice to change the state of machinery, objects and enemies, opening routes through the factory to carry out repairs.
Goals
Use freezing to repair equipment, then use that equipment to get past obstacles and reach the next area.
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Genre
3D action puzzle
Project duration
14 days
Team & production
Solo project. I designed the puzzles and routes, built the blockout and implemented interactions in Blueprints.

The repair route

The control system has failed. Only the backup lights are on, steam is leaking into the factory, and pressure is building beneath a shut-down well. The workers have evacuated. The engineer arrives by helicopter and follows the lights through the deserted living quarters to the control room.

From the control-room window, the player can see part of the rig and the large robot damaging it. The door to the platform is locked. I wanted the player to see where they were going before setting out to fix the pipes, find the key and restore power.

Ice and industry

I used the shrines in The Legend of Zelda as a reference for a compact level built around learning an ability. Here, ice is the engineer’s tool. A steam vent blocks a doorway, a machine part blocks a walkway, and a robot gives chase. Freezing each of them has a different result.

Teaching the freezing beam

For each mechanic, I broke the puzzles into an introduction, a chance to practise, and an application. Each step keeps one piece of knowledge the player can reason from. To practise an old rule, I change the obstacle. To teach a new use, I first give the player a familiar obstacle to solve with it.

A single steam leak

The beam pickup sits beside a doorway blocked by steam. Aim at the vent and freeze it. The jet stops and the path opens. From here the player can already see the next ability pickup, farther inside the factory.

The object blocking the walkway

After another stretch of single-pipe practice, the player finds a blocked walkway and a vent that is already frozen. This time the jet is needed. Freeze the object first, then thaw the vent. The steam pushes the frozen object out of the way.

Two vents on the same pipe

Seal the near vent and the jet from the upper one reaches farther. Both vents are accessible here, so the player can freeze them in turn. I put this room before the combined puzzle so the player can see how one vent affects the other.

Using pressure to reach the chest

A chest is visible beyond the blockage. After the first vent is sealed, the second jet reaches across the route, but the player cannot aim at that vent from here. They have to adjust the connected pipe and use steam to move the object before they can reach the chest and freeze the vent.

I counted the shortest solutions for three of these puzzles: one action for a single leak, three for the blocked walkway, and five for the chest. The extra steps come from choosing which object to freeze and deciding when a vent needs to be open again.

From puzzles to combat

I placed the first robot before the cooling ability pickup. The freezing beam tells the player the robot is too hot. What they can already do is move objects with steam. Pushing an object off the walkway above crushes the robot. Where it lands, it also forms the step the player needs to climb out.

Cooling, freezing and a finishing kick

The player reaches the pickup they saw beside the first vent. Ice projectiles lower a robot’s heat until the freezing prompt appears. The beam stops it in place, giving the player time to move in for a finishing kick.

I first put two robots in an L-shaped corridor. As the player freezes the one in front, the second comes around the corner and into the line of the kick. The next encounter has the same two opponents in an open room. The player has seen what a collision does. Now they have to move around the frozen robot to find that angle themselves.

The pipe maze and power station

With three vents on a pipe, a state that opens the current passage can block the next one. The player has to go back and thaw vents they used on the way in. In the maze, two separate three-vent circuits cross the route. I put a raised lookout at the entrance so the player can see how the pipes are grouped before walking between them.

Early on, a one-way door is visible from the side that will not open. The route takes the player round to change the far vents, open the door from behind and return to the earlier controls. At the fork beyond, the lower factory is locked. The other path leads to the power station.

One robot guards the narrow entrance. The next fight opens out around the key and chest. Here a second window gives the player a much closer view of the drilling platform. In the elite encounter, obstacles provide cover between shots. Restoring power and taking the key back to the fork opens the route downstairs.

Freezing the player

I first let the player walk along the bank. Falling into the chemical pool sends them back, but there is still a dry route. At the next pool the gap is too wide to jump. Looking up reveals a familiar movable object and steam pipe. Freeze the object and knock it into the liquid to make a bridge.

Turning a corner on the water

Only after the player understands the pool do I give them the ability to freeze themselves. In this state they float but cannot steer. The first jet carries them across to the back of a one-way door. At the curved pool, the first jet must leave them where a second one can carry them around the bend.

A lift brings the upper vents and the blocked wall-side walkway back within reach. After clearing the obstruction, the player returns to the pool to cross on three jets. At the largest pool, two linked vents knock an object into the liquid. The player steps onto it, freezes themselves and rides the next three jets across. The bridge made in the first half is the starting position for the second.

Back to the drilling platform

Clearing the next upper obstruction brings the route back to the entrance of the upper factory. A lift returns the player to the control room, where the route to the drill is finally open. The engineer clears the platform and freezes the failing drill.

For the final encounter, I brought back the falling object and the finishing kick. The player cools and freezes the boss, then climbs the crane. A kick sends the last frozen robot into an object held above the platform and knocks it down onto the boss. Back in the control room, the engineer restores monitoring and clears the alarm.

Building the prototype

In 14 days I designed the areas, routes and puzzle order, built the blockout, and implemented and debugged the interactions in Unreal Blueprints. I focused on freezing, linked steam vents, movable objects, combat states and self-freezing. The companion robot, synchronised gears, factory-wide lighting change and a second boss phase remained in the design document.

I used the UE5 Third Person template, Starter Content, Blockout Tools and a low-poly treasure chest. I used AI to check the sequence, discuss Blueprint logic and errors, and help with visual references and the document structure.
