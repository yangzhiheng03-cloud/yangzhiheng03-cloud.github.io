Offshore Factory

2026.06
Overview
Restore a damaged offshore factory with a freezing beam. Seal pipes to redirect steam, move machinery into place and cross hazardous pools.
Goals
Teach one rule through successive repairs. Once the player has used steam to push an obstacle, can they recognise a place where the same force could carry them across a gap?
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Genre
3D action puzzle
Project duration
14 days
Team & production
Solo project. I designed the puzzles and routes, built the blockout and implemented interactions in Blueprints.

Making pressure visible

A jet blocks the first passage. Freezing the outlet clears it. The next puzzle connects two outlets: sealing one extends the jet from the other. Early setups keep cause and effect in one view. Later ones ask the player to turn around or look across floors.

One obstacle, two changes to the route

The first overheated robot resists the freezing beam. The player can freeze an object above it and push it off a ledge with steam. The falling object defeats the robot and becomes a step to the next level. A later ice projectile introduces a direct sequence of cooling, freezing and finishing an enemy.

When the player becomes the moving object

The lower pool first asks the player to push an object into the liquid to make a foothold. Freezing the character then allows floating at the cost of steering. The last crossing combines both rules: build a stop midway, line up with the jet and let it carry the frozen character across.
