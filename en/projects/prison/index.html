Prison: Introductory Level

2026.03
Overview
The player is a prisoner in a mountaintop prison. With help from a companion outside, they escape their cell and search the prison for evidence of human experiments. The escape takes them through the warden’s office and into the laboratory.
Goals
Teach the basics of combat and puzzle-solving through the level’s core loop: fight, explore, solve a puzzle, then fight again.
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Genre
Soulslike tutorial level
Project duration
14 days
Team & production
Solo project. I designed the level flow, enemy encounters and looping routes, and built the blockout.

Level structure

I designed the prison as an 81 × 74 m level with one main route, for a first playthrough of 20–30 minutes.

The planned loop is combat, exploration, a puzzle, then combat again. Across 36 beats I planned seven regular encounters, two elite encounters, one boss fight and two puzzles. A safe room or a quieter stretch of exploration follows each high-pressure encounter.

The cell block: sword, shield and backstab

A knock at the cell door

The prisoner wakes to the sound of a huge creature fighting. A body is visible through the window. A scrap of paper on the floor reads, “Knock on the door when you’re ready.” The player can move around the cell and decide when to knock.

The door opens. A guard comes to investigate. Just before the guard can strike, a sword flies in and runs him through. A voice urges the prisoner to leave. Further down the corridor, a cell door crashes to the floor. The player picks up the sword as another enemy approaches. I wanted the player to know that someone outside was helping, and to give that first weapon a reason to be there.

The beast outside the window

At the far end of the cell block lies a body with deep wounds. A shield lies beside it. For a moment the beast is just outside the window. It moves away, the music settles, and the voice gives the player a destination. The beast is the product of an experiment. The research records are in the warden’s office. The player takes the shield into the torture room for the next fight.

Two guards looking the other way

Beyond the torture room, bars block one route and stairs lead up to the guards’ quarters. Two guards have their backs to the stairs. They are watching the beast through holes in the wall. The player can backstab one and fight the other alone. Further on, two more guards hear the noise and arrive together. This is the first time the player has to handle two opponents at once.

The first safe room

After the fight, a broken bridge stops the player. Beside a chest at the break, they can see the boss in full for the first time. A passage and a flight of stairs lead to the first safe room, where the player can recover and collect a healing item.

I put four enemies in the narrow lookout passage. The first faces away, giving the player the backstab they already know. That leaves three enemies in very little space. This was the encounter most likely to send a beginner back to the safe room. I kept the three opponents and put the respawn point right beside the challenge. A failed attempt costs a short walk, so the player can try the fight again straight away.

A ladder at the far end leads down to a room with cannons facing out of the prison. The first elite enemy stands in front of a large chest. The player can fight for the chest, or take the passage beside the ladder and leave the encounter for later.

The chapel and the far side of the bridge

The second safe room sits beneath the chapel. After resting, the player follows a side passage to the front of the chapel, collects an item, then reaches the opposite side of the broken bridge. The boss is below. This time there is a choice: drop down and attempt the fight early, or stay on the route to the warden’s office.

I changed the player’s view of the same creature over three encounters. At the cell window it is a threat that passes. From the first side of the bridge, seeing its whole body gives the player a longer-term goal. On the opposite side, looking down gives the player a chance to act on that goal.

The warden’s office and the route back

A key on the desk

A floor mechanism at the office entrance stays shut on the first visit. Inside, picking up the key on the desk moves the bed aside and reveals a passage underneath. The player has now seen what a key can do to the room itself. A ladder along this passage leads back to the first safe room.

A chest above, four enemies below

Below the safe room an enemy waits around a corner. The player can see a chest through a gap overhead, but cannot reach it from here. A little further on, a ladder overlooks four enemies. The player can see their positions before committing to the descent. After the fight, a gallery with a view outside gives the player a breather.

The experiment and the escape

Past the gallery guard, the player can see a creature through a narrow gap. It resembles the boss on a much smaller scale. This is the clue that the giant was once human. The room just ahead is on the other side of the bars the player passed after the torture room.

Defeating this enemy gives the player a bomb. They climb to the room above and plant it among the materials used in the experiments. Past the next door, an ambush guards a chest containing the key to the office floor mechanism.

The corridor comes out at the chest the player saw overhead. Dropping through the gap takes them back to the office. I shortened the run from finding the evidence to confronting the boss by making this last shortcut part of the laboratory entrance. Beneath the floor mechanism are broken chains and the final fight.

After defeating the boss, the player walks out through the prison gate. For the first time, the whole mountain landscape opens up around them.
