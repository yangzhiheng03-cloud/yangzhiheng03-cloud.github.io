Prison: Introductory Level

  • UE5
  • Soulslike
  • Tutorial level
  • Looping routes

I mapped this escape across 36 beats, targeting 20–30 minutes. The player starts in a cell with room to practise movement, learns to fight, discovers shortcuts, and eventually returns to the laboratory glimpsed earlier.

Introducing tools just before they matter

Knocking draws a guard. An incoming sword saves the prisoner and gives the first weapon a place in the story. A single opponent lets the player try it. A shield found beside a body leads into blocking and backstabs before the first two-on-one encounter tests those skills together.

Keeping retries close to the challenge

The broken bridge reveals the boss, followed by a safe room that lets the tension fall. The next narrow space sets up a three-on-one encounter, close enough to retry quickly after defeat. An optional elite farther along gives the player a choice about how much danger to take on.

A locked mechanism gives the return trip a purpose

A mechanism in the warden’s office cannot be opened on the first visit. A related puzzle teaches its logic. The route loops back with a key item and access to a previously unreachable chest. A shortcut to the laboratory shortens later boss attempts and reconnects the discovery to the escape story.
